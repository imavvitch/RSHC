The build needs retoc.exe, a free tool that writes the container files Unreal 5.6 games load.

1. Download the latest Windows release from https://github.com/trumank/retoc/releases
2. Put retoc.exe in this folder (next to this note).

Or keep it anywhere and pass its place to the build:
    BUILD.cmd -Retoc "C:\Tools\retoc\retoc.exe"

retoc is not part of RSHC and is not included in this kit.
